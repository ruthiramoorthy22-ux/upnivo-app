Delete your UPNIVO INTERN account

This page lets you delete your account for the UPNIVO INTERN app (Upnivo, Android package com.upnivo.app) and the website, without installing the app. You can also delete your account inside the app: Profile → Delete my account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, mobile number, college, degree, department, year, city, state, gender, about text and photo.
  • Your password. The account is closed and can no longer sign in.
  • Your notifications and your push-notification registrations.
  • Your task submissions' content: links, GitHub and demo links, notes and screenshots (screenshots stored on our server are removed).

What is kept, and why

  • Minimal enrollment records (which internship, plan, status) so that issued certificates and reports stay consistent.
  • Certificate-fee payment records (reference, amount, status) needed for accounting and refunds.
  • Security and audit log entries.

These records are no longer linked to your name, email or phone number.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Cannot remember your password? See the contact details on the Help page of the app: Help & support.

## 3. Domain
Configured host: `upnivo-intern.onrender.com` (from your brief). **Not verified live** (sandbox cannot reach it).

Consequences of the domain:
- The host is baked into the Android app (asset statements, launch URL, trusted origin) and into `assetlinks.json`. **Changing the domain later requires a new app release**, and old installs keep opening the old host.
- Digital Asset Links must be served from the same host. A redirect from the old to the new host is not enough for verification.
- Cookies/localStorage (the login token) are per origin: users would have to log in again on a new domain.
- Render's free tier may sleep when idle, so the first launch can take many seconds; a paid always-on instance or a custom domain on a paid plan is recommended before real users.
- **Recommendation: buy and attach a custom domain on Render before the first Play release**, then regenerate the TWA with that host. This is a business/DNS decision; nothing was changed or purchased.
